01 — About

The Full Picture

I'm a final year B.Tech undergraduate in Electrical & Computer Engineering at MBCET — and simultaneously a founder, a COO, a diplomat, a researcher, and a guitarist.

At 19, I founded the International Matrix Foundation, building it into an internationally recognized academic simulation enterprise with partnerships spanning the IIT ecosystem, European, and Southeast Asian universities. I serve as COO at ProBiz Knowledge Centre and as Ambassador for India at International Diplomats (UAE).

My work lives at the convergence of technical engineering (power electronics, IoT, quantitative modeling) and global governance: international environmental law, biosecurity, disarmament, and human rights mechanisms. I've chaired over 45+ high-level committees at premier institutions including IIT Bombay, IIIT Guwahati, and IIT Roorkee to name a few.

Off the circuit: I've played guitar for 11+ years, game Call of Duty for 5 years and I'm deep into organizational project governance as the next frontier I want to work in.
